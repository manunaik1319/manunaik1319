## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🏢 Zyvo

**Zyvo is a platform for discovering and booking study spaces.**

The goal is to make it easier for students to find suitable **study halls, reading rooms, libraries, and study spaces** based on factors such as:

* 📍 Location
* 💰 Pricing
* 🪑 Availability
* 🤫 Study environment
* ⭐ Ratings & information
* 📅 Membership options

**Current focus:** Hyderabad → Telangana → India

---

### 🎯 Rankit

A student-focused platform designed to help students make better decisions around **college selection and admission planning**.
